# App names: shortlist and zero-cost selection plan

Prepared 13 September 2026. Brand has been set to **Deenstead** for this validation cycle.

## Recommendation

**Test Deenstead first, Salahside second, and Veyami as the deliberately less literal alternative.**

Deenstead has the best balance of recognisable faith context, steadiness, and room to expand beyond prayer reminders. Salahside is easier to understand immediately and fits a prayer-led first release. Veyami offers a compact, internationally styled brand, but needs a clear Muslim-app descriptor everywhere it appears.

My preferred provisional lockup:

> **Deenstead**  
> Prayer, Qibla & daily practice  
> Make room for faith, every day.

This is a creative recommendation, not a claim that a name is available to register. The preliminary screening below found no obvious exact-name Muslim app for these three in the searches performed; it did find other uses and several reasons to reject tempting alternatives. No domain, trademark, company-name, or social-handle availability has been established.

## Naming brief

The name should feel welcoming to a busy Muslim returning to consistent practice. It should work when someone says, “I use ___ for prayer times and a small daily reminder.” It should remain appropriate for Qur’an-based reflection, character-building, and private journaling.

Prefer easy pronunciation, ordinary keyboard spelling, warmth, and a reasonably distinctive search term. Avoid promises of religious authority, guaranteed spiritual transformation, perfection, or a score of how good a Muslim someone is. “Muslim” and practical keywords can live in the descriptor; the brand does not need to contain the entire feature list.

Both internationally styled coinages and recognisably Islamic combinations are worth testing. A preference for neutral software names in another category should not decide what feels trustworthy to this audience.

The combinations below are original naming proposals, **not established Arabic phrases**. Where used, *deen* refers to religion/faith in this naming context, and *salah* to prayer; their underlying Arabic words have contextual meanings. The [Quranic Arabic Corpus entries for dīn](https://corpus.quran.com/qurandictionary.jsp?q=dyn) and [ṣalāh](https://corpus.quran.com/qurandictionary.jsp?q=Slw) provide linguistic context. Have native speakers and the religious reviewer assess any final hybrid name.

## Thirty ideas across three directions

Only the five finalists below received focused collision screening. The remaining ideas are an exploratory pool, not cleared recommendations.

### A. Faith-recognisable combinations

| Name | Intended association | Main reservation |
|---|---|---|
| **Deenstead** | A steady place for daily faith | “Stead” may be unfamiliar outside English-speaking markets |
| **Salahside** | A companion alongside prayer | May sound limited to prayer utility |
| **QuietDeen** | Private, calm daily practice | Could imply a meditation-only product |
| **Deenwoven** | Faith woven through ordinary life | Longer and less natural to say |
| EverSalah | Continuity and returning to prayer | “Ever” can suggest constant performance |
| Deenloom | Small practices woven together | Spaced form has a historical loom-company collision; lower priority |
| Salahseed | Small beginnings that grow | Could feel like a children’s app |
| DeenHarbor | A welcoming place to return | US/UK harbor/harbour spelling split |
| DeenThread | Continuity through the day | May suggest clothing or a discussion feed |
| SalahDaily | Immediately practical | Generic and harder to distinguish |

## Five finalists

| Rank | Name and pronunciation | Why it fits | Honest risk | Suggested short line |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Deenstead** — DEEN-sted | Suggests steadiness and a place to return; broad enough for prayer and daily conduct | English compound needs explanation; an unrelated music username already uses the string | Make room for faith, every day. |
| 2 | **Salahside** — suh-LAH-side | Immediately connects the brand to prayer and companionship; memorable spoken form | Understates Qur’an and character content; “salah” spelling/pronunciation varies by audience | Prayer and practice, side by side. |
| 3 | **Veyami** — vay-AH-mee | Short and flexible; does not borrow a sacred term or imply authority | Existing jewellery product name; category meaning is absent; “v” and pronunciation need testing | Your daily Muslim companion. |
| 4 | **QuietDeen** — KWY-et DEEN | Communicates calm and discretion directly | Could be mistaken for meditation or imply that religious practice itself should be quiet | A little space for daily faith. |
| 5 | **Deenwoven** — DEEN-woh-vuhn | Closely matches the idea of faith shaping ordinary life | More syllables and spelling friction; meaning may land only after explanation | Faith, woven into your day. |

Deenstead is the broadest practical choice for the current concept. Choose Salahside if interviews show prayer consistency is the dominant reason people install. Choose Veyami only if its recall and trust results justify the extra work of explaining the brand.

Do not claim “scholar-reviewed,” “private,” or “ad-free” in final store copy until the corresponding review and product requirements are met. Naming is not evidence that the experience delivers those qualities.

## Preliminary collision screen

Checked 13 September 2026 using live quoted-name web searches, name-plus-app queries, selected spaced/phonetic variants, and searches scoped to `apps.apple.com`. This is an **indexed-search screen**, not an exhaustive search of every App Store storefront. A missing indexed result can reflect indexing gaps.

| Finalist | Search observations | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Deenstead | Exact-name and Apple-scoped searches did not surface an obvious app. An unrelated [Audiotool creator handle](https://www.audiotool.com/track/beat_this) uses the name. | Keep for user testing; investigate broader commercial and phonetic conflicts before launch |
| Salahside | Exact, “Salah Side,” and Apple-scoped searches did not surface an obvious exact-name app. Some results joined unrelated “Salah” and “Side” text. | Keep; compare recognition against the crowded Salah-prefix category |
| Veyami | No obvious exact-name app surfaced in the Apple-scoped searches. [Leyane sells earrings named Veyami](https://leyane.com/products/boucles-d-oreilles-veya). | Keep as a creative alternative, with an explicit existing commercial-use warning |
| QuietDeen | Exact, spaced, and Apple-scoped searches did not surface an obvious exact-name app; an unrelated gaming username appeared. | Keep; validate religious and emotional connotations |
| Deenwoven | Exact-name, app, and Apple-scoped searches returned no obvious exact-name product. | Keep; clarity and pronunciation remain the main creative concerns |

Recorded query patterns: `"Deenstead"`, `"Salahside"`, `"Veyami"`, `"QuietDeen"`, `"Deenwoven"`; each with `app` and `site:apps.apple.com`; selected variants included `"Deen Stead"`, `"Salah Side"`, and `"Quiet Deen"`. Negative findings above describe this research session, not legal availability.

### Attractive names rejected or deprioritised

| Name | Evidence and reason |
|---|---|
| Niyyah / close variations | [Niyyah](https://www.niyyah.app/) already occupies a very similar daily Islamic practice position; multiple other Niyyah apps also exist. Reject. |
| Dayward | Existing [daily planning software](https://dayward.ai/) and a [spiritual companion](https://getdayward.com/). Reject. |
| Stillstep | [Still Step is already an iPhone wellbeing app](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/still-step/id6754638479). Spacing does not solve the confusion. Reject. |
| Dailora | Existing [habit-tracking app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lxt.dailora). Reject. |
| Steadya | Near-homophone of [Steadia, “Understand your days” software](https://steadia.io/). Reject from this shortlist. |
| Deenward | Existing [Deenward education business](https://www.deenward.com/). Reject. |
| Seluvo | Existing [technical system from Ishikawa](https://www.kk-ishikawa.jp/g2025111201.html) and other commercial uses. Deprioritise. |
| Deenloom | The spaced form names a historical [Deen Loom Company museum artefact](https://www.thehenryford.org/collections/explore/artifact/449343). Not an app collision, but additional screening work with little advantage over Deenstead. |

Also avoid starting from bare Noor/Nur, Deen, Sabr, Sukoon, Ihsan, or names belonging to the competitors in the market report. A familiar religious word alone is unlikely to make discovery easier in this category.

The Niyyah finding also affects positioning: “a daily lesson ending in an action” cannot be advertised as unique. The product needs a narrower, validated reason to choose it.

## A 48-hour, zero-cash name test

**Day 1:** Make identical text-only cards for Deenstead, Salahside, and Veyami, each with the same descriptor: “Prayer times, Qibla, and a small daily Islamic practice.” No logo variations, colours, or founder explanation that could bias the choice.

Recruit around 15 consenting adults from the intended audience through existing relationships or community channels that expressly allow research. Include different English fluency levels and a mix of Arabic, Urdu, and other language backgrounds. Do not imply a religious identity or expertise the founder does not have. Do not use unsolicited mass DMs.

Ask, in rotating order:

1. What would you expect this app to do?
2. How would you pronounce the name?
3. Hearing it once without seeing it, how would you spell it?
4. Which feels most trustworthy, and why?
5. Does any name feel awkward, misleading, childish, or religiously uncomfortable?
6. After ten minutes, which names can you remember without seeing them?

**Day 2:** Record exact explanations with permission, not just favourite-name votes. As practical decision rules, aim for at least 12 of 15 participants to spell the leading name correctly after hearing it, and no repeated serious religious concern. These are founder-set usability targets, not statistical validation. Prefer the name people remember and understand over the name that receives the most polite compliments.

Before committing, check relevant trademark registers, similar spellings and sounds, App Store and Google Play storefronts, domains, and major social handles for the actual launch markets. Obtain appropriate professional clearance when feasible. If a serious conflict remains, keep **Deenstead** while testing the product; a paid domain or finished logo is unnecessary for the first learning cycle.
