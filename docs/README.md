## Recommended direction

**Name to test first: Deenstead.** Alternatives: Salahside for immediate prayer relevance, Veyami for an abstract brand, QuietDeen for a calmer tone, and Deenwoven for daily-life positioning. These have preliminary indexed-search screening, not trademark or domain clearance. The naming pool includes an internationally styled direction in line with earlier naming feedback, without assuming the same preferences must govern a faith app.

**First product:** a free, self-paced 14-day *Reconnect With Salah* journey, prayer times, Qibla, optional reminders, a basic widget and private local reflections. No mandatory account, advertising, public worship scores or AI religious adviser.

**First monetization:** a finished additional journey at a proposed $7.99 one-time price. Consider $5.99/month or $39.99/year only after a useful reviewed library and ongoing service exist. Keep prayer essentials and the starter free. All prices require testing; there is no revenue forecast.

**First distribution:** 15–20 interviews, a permission-based pilot reaching 20 activated participants, relevant developer/tester communities, and one opted-in email or Telegram channel. Budget roughly eight founder marketing hours per week. Prepare Product Hunt after a stable release is available; buy neither votes nor a hunter.

## Findings that change the plan

- **Daily teachings plus actions already have a close competitor.** Niyyah offers this combination. The narrower restart-friendly proposition still needs a real reason for users to choose it. [Current developer listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.guider.guidermobile)
- **Reddit reach is constrained.** r/islam and r/converts prohibit promotion/recruitment; r/MuslimLounge's approval criteria do not fit the proposed paid model. Use the live-rule table in the marketing plan; a large subscriber count does not create permission to post.
- **Official Telegram Ads is unsuitable under current rules.** Its policy prohibits promoting religious practices/rituals. Use organic partnerships. A disclosed direct channel sponsorship is only a conditional, separately checked option; do not conceal the app's purpose to bypass ad review. [Telegram policy](https://ads.telegram.org/guidelines)
- **$0 marketing is possible; a native release may still cost money.** Apple membership is normally $99/year with regional pricing, unless already covered or a waiver applies. Qualified content review and rights also need to be secured. [Apple enrollment](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/)

## First seven days

1. Test Deenstead, Salahside and Veyami with consenting target users; do not purchase branding assets yet.
2. Conduct five problem interviews and compare the concept against people's existing tools.
3. Find a qualified reviewer willing to discuss a narrowly scoped pilot; confirm availability and terms.
4. Prepare three simple screens and one rights-cleared, reviewed sample if review is available. Otherwise use clearly labeled interface placeholders.
5. Check permitted recruitment routes and invite explicit opt-in. Do not post promotional drafts to prohibited communities.

The copy skill shaped the outcome-led messaging and the distinction between actual review evidence and hypotheses. Current Apple notification documentation informed the PRD's permission and scheduling requirements. The next decision is whether the narrow routine earns repeat use; a polished all-in-one feature list does not answer that question.
